    A helpful estimate, not a hard rule.

    We use the Flesch Reading Ease formula and a transparent set of sentence signals. Syllables are estimated, and fiction, dialogue, names, and fragments can confuse the math. This is a starting point for revision—not a verdict on your prose.

    A little more on the method ↗
    How the score and sentence suggestions work

    Reading ease: 206.835 − 1.015 × (words ÷ sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables ÷ words). Higher scores generally suggest easier reading. Scores are clipped to the familiar 0–100 range.

    Sentence suggestions: sentences are ranked using length, estimated syllables per word, selected punctuation, and subordinate-clause signals. We show up to five, with a short explanation of the signals behind each one. These weights are practical heuristics, not a validated measure of comprehension.

    Privacy: analysis runs in this browser. Chapter text is not sent to a server, stored, or used for analytics.
